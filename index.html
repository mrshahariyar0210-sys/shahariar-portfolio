03
Water Conservation Model

Estimate ecosystem service capacity using the Water Conservation model.

Formula · SWC Index
SWC = NPP × Fsic × Fpre × (1 − Fslo) × (1 − Fsal)

The Soil & Water Conservation (SWC) index combines vegetation productivity with soil, rainfall, slope and salinity factors to estimate how well a land unit retains water and prevents erosion — higher values indicate stronger conservation capacity.

  • SWCSoil & Water Conservation index (output raster)
  • NPPNet Primary Productivity — vegetation growth capacity (MOD17A3HGF)
  • FsicSoil infiltration factor, derived from clay content (SoilGrids)
  • FprePrecipitation factor, normalized mean rainfall (CHIRPS)
  • FsloSlope factor — steeper slopes reduce conservation capacity (SRTM DEM)
  • FsalSalinity factor — higher salinity reduces conservation capacity (FAO GSAS)
🌿 NPP — Net Primary Productivity 🪨 Fsic — Soil Infiltration Factor 🌧 Fpre — Precipitation Factor ⛰ Fslo — Slope Factor 🧂 Fsal — Salinity Factor
Input Factors→Raster Calculation→SWC Layer→Water Conservation Map
Output: SWC Raster

04
Soil Conservation Model

Estimate ecosystem service capacity using the Soil Conservation model.

Formula · Soil Conservation Index
SSC = NPP × (1 − Fslo) × (1 − Kfactor)

The Soil Conservation (SSC) index combines vegetation productivity with slope and soil erodibility factors to estimate how well a land unit retains soil and resists erosion — higher values indicate stronger soil conservation capacity.

  • SSCSoil Conservation index (output raster)
  • NPPNet Primary Productivity — vegetation growth capacity (MOD17A3HGF)
  • FsloSlope factor — steeper slopes reduce conservation capacity (SRTM DEM)
  • KfactorSoil erodibility factor — higher erodibility reduces conservation capacity (SoilGrids)
🌿 NPP — Net Primary Productivity ⛰ Fslo — Slope Factor 🪨 Kfactor — Soil Erodibility Factor
Input Factors→Raster Calculation→SSC Layer→Soil Conservation Map
Output: SSC Raster
Spatial Modelling Raster Calculator
05
Biodiversity Conservation Model

Estimate ecosystem service capacity using the Biodiversity Conservation model.

Formula · Biodiversity Conservation Index
Sbio = NPP × Fpre × Ftem × (1 − Falt)

The Biodiversity Conservation (Sbio) index combines vegetation productivity with rainfall, temperature and elevation factors to estimate habitat suitability — higher values indicate areas of greater ecological importance for biodiversity.

  • SbioBiodiversity Conservation index (output raster)
  • NPPNet Primary Productivity — vegetation growth capacity (MOD17A3HGF)
  • FprePrecipitation factor, normalized mean rainfall (CHIRPS)
  • FtemTemperature factor, normalized mean temperature (WorldClim)
  • FaltAltitude factor — higher elevation reduces biodiversity conservation capacity (SRTM DEM)
🌿 NPP — Net Primary Productivity 🌧 Fpre — Precipitation Factor 🌡 Ftem — Temperature Factor ⛰ Falt — Altitude Factor
Input Factors→Raster Calculation→Sbio Layer→Biodiversity Conservation Map
